Frequently Asked Questions

How does one-pot pasta work?

The pasta cooks directly in the sauce and stock instead of separately drained water. As it simmers it absorbs the liquid and releases starch, which thickens everything into a glossy sauce — in one pan, with one wash-up.

How much liquid per serving?

About 200 ml of stock plus ~90 g of dry pasta per serving. The generator scales both with your serving count. If the pan looks dry before the pasta is tender, add a splash more hot water.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Completely free, no sign-up, no email. It runs in your browser and works offline once the page has loaded.

Can I trust an auto-generated recipe?

The ratios and pasta times come from standard cooking references, not guesses, and the sauce pairings are hand-picked. We're transparent that recipes are auto-generated and not individually kitchen-tested — use your own judgement and a thermometer for meat.
